What is SWOT Analysis?

SWOT analysis is a strategic planning tool used to evaluate Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats related to a decision or problem.

💪 Strengths

Internal advantages that help you succeed.

  • Strong brand recognition
  • Skilled employees
  • Efficient operations
  • Unique technology

⚠️ Weaknesses

Internal challenges that could hold you back.

  • Limited resources
  • Outdated systems
  • Poor visibility
  • High costs

🚀 Opportunities

External trends or possibilities you can take advantage of.

  • Market growth
  • New partnerships
  • Emerging technology
  • Changing regulations

⚡ Threats

External risks or obstacles you should prepare for.

  • New competitors
  • Regulatory change
  • Economic shifts
  • Technology disruption

How to Do a SWOT Analysis

Follow these steps to create your own SWOT analysis.

  1. Define the problem or decision. Clarify the goal or challenge you want to explore.
  2. Identify internal strengths. List what you do well and what gives you an advantage.
  3. Identify internal weaknesses. Be honest about what holds you back.
  4. Look for external opportunities. Find trends or situations you can use.
  5. Identify external threats. Note risks that could affect your plan.
  6. Use insights to plan actions. Turn your findings into clear next steps.
